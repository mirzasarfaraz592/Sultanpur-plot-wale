FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the plots legally verified and Dakhil Kharij ready?
Yes, all our plots have 100% clear titles and are completely eligible for Dakhil Kharij. We perform extensive legal due diligence before listing any property to ensure a safe investment for our buyers.
What plot sizes are available?
We offer a variety of standard plot dimensions including 1000, 1250, 1500, and 2000 sq ft. We also offer larger options for farm lands depending on the location.
What amenities are provided in the developed plots?
Our premium plots come fully developed with 30ft to 40ft wide Damar roads, proper drainage (Nali), electricity poles (Bijli Kambha), boundary walls (in select locations), and easy access to nearby hospitals, schools, and markets.
Can I get immediate possession?
Absolutely. Once the booking and registry is complete, you get instant possession of your plot and can start construction immediately.
Do you arrange site visits and is there a fee?
We provide 100% FREE guided site visits with transport assistance. Simply contact us via WhatsApp or phone to schedule an appointment at your convenience.
